## Founder-led storytelling reels

For any founder story reel, ad with a founder's VO, or "story / hook / reel" job:
1. Read `founder-led-storytelling/PLAYBOOK.md` first and follow its gates. Nothing gets generated before Enjo approves the brief (gate 1) and the edit plan (gate 2).
2. Copy the templates and logs into `founder-led-storytelling/projects/<client>-<reel>/`.
3. Use `library/` for prompts, slop risks, intake questions and estimates.
4. At the end of every review round, add lessons to `LESSONS.md` and propose any rule changes. Never edit the standing rules without Enjo's yes.
