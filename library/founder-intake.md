# Founder intake questions

Send these before starting, or fill them in from the brief and flag any gaps. The answers feed the Story brief.

**The story**
1. What happened, in your own words? Tell us one moment you remember clearly.
2. What changed because of it? What did you decide or do differently?
3. Is the story finished (resolved), or are you still living it?

**Real material** (real material always beats AI)
4. Photos from that time: people, places, objects. Even blurry ones help.
5. Video of you, your workplace or your product today.
6. One object from the story you still have or can describe (for the visual anchor).
7. The real places: home, shop, office, any location that appears in the story.

**How you talk**
8. Words or phrases you always use, including local expressions. We keep them.
9. Anything you'd never say or show.

**Off-limits and approvals**
10. People who can't appear (only hands, or not at all)? Topics to avoid?
11. Who approves the video, and how fast do they usually reply?
12. Tracking codes, ad account naming, or posting captions we have to use?

**Delivery**
13. Platform(s) and lengths. Organic, ad, or both? What CTA and offer?
14. Deadline and number of hook variants.
