# Worked example · Tahi Repair Co. (fictional)

*The founder, business and story are invented to test the playbook.* Starting point: **B · finished script**, so every timecode is **EST** at about 2.2 words per second until the VO is recorded.

## Story brief

**Reel:** Tahi 01, "I Closed the Refund Drawer" · 60 s · **Gate 1:** draft for Enjo

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Founder** | Dara Quinto, 34, founder of Tahi Repair Co. in Cebu City: backpacks sold with free lifetime repair. Speaks plainly with a dry humour, and slips in Bisaya ("sige," "lagi"). Keep those words. |
| **Audience** | Students and commuters in their 20s who buy cheap bags every year and assume that breaking is normal |
| **Source material** | Script (below). Real: shop counter, the actual refund drawer, repair bench footage, a photo of her father on his boat (1990s), his bamboo net-mending needle. Missing: the student's visit, which has to be illustrated |
| **Story arc** | 1 · She resold cheap bags online and refunded every broken strap without asking why · 2 · 200 refunds pile up in one drawer · 3 · A student brings the same bag back a third time and asks for a fix, not a refund · 4 · Dara takes down her father's net needle, sews the strap, and closes the refund drawer for good. **Resolved** |
| **Turning point** | Her hand is on the open refund drawer, reaching for a slip. She **pushes the drawer shut** and lifts the needle off its nail instead. The decision happens in the action, with no words needed |
| **Visual anchor** | **Her father's bamboo net needle.** First seen in the background on its nail (beat 1), taken down at the turn (beat 3), and in a new hire's hand at the close |
| **Intended ending** | Organic: "Things don't have to be thrown away. They just need someone who stays." Final image: a new staff member threads the same needle. Ad: + CTA "Bring your broken bag to our Cebu shop this month. We'll fix it free, even if it isn't ours." |
| Hooks (×3) | H1 "I gave out 200 refunds before a student told me no." · H2 "My father fixed fishing nets for 30 years. I almost forgot how." · H3 "The worst business advice I followed: just refund them." |
| Bridge | "Let me tell you about that drawer." |
| Open questions for Enjo | 1. Should the student appear on screen, or only her hands and the bag? 2. Is the H2 hook too close to the ending? 3. Does she wear the same shop apron in every scene? |
